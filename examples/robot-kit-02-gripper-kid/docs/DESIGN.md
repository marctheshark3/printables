# robot-kit-02-gripper

Narrative only. The machine contract is `PRINT_SPEC.yaml`.

Kid-friendly 02 claw-bot: a little robot with a body and a claw. Enclosed rounded torso and lid, MCU under a window, 02 on the chest. One arm comes out of a right-shoulder fairing. A forearm sleeve wraps the wrist SG90 so you never see a naked servo brick. Two red curved pincer fingers meet like a crab. Assembly pose (preview): arm up and slightly forward, claws slightly open.

Base SG90 stays in the torso pocket (`sg90_pocket_void`). Distal 8 mm horn plate lives inside the sleeve. Nested wrist servo omitted from occupancy; MCU and base SG90 stay in the occupancy set.

Print: hull, lid, sleeve, finger. Buy MCU, two SG90s, battery.
