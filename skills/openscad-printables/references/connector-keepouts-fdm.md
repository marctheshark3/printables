# FDM case connector keepouts

Quick reference for wall cutouts. Prefer calipers on the actual board when available.

| Connector | Typical plug/housing | Recommended wall window (W×H mm) | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------------|--------|
| micro-USB Type-B | ~10.6 × 8.5 shell | **13.0 × 8.5** | Overmold + insert angle |
| USB-C receptacle | ~9 × 3.2 | **12 × 7** | More height for overmold cables |
| microSD card path | card 15×11×1 | **16.0 × 3.5** | Slot housing taller than card |
| STEMMA QT / JST-SH 4p | ~6–8 mm | **10 × 8** one / **22 × 9** pair | Cable bend |
| 2.1 mm barrel DC | Ø5.5 plug | **Ø8–9** or 9×9 square | Strain relief |
| SMA / RP-SMA | nut flats | **Ø8+** panel | Don’t trap nut inside wall |

Always state z0 from floor (board stack height). Wrong edge assignment is worse than slightly large windows.
