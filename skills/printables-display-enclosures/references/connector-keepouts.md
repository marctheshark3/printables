# FDM case connector keepouts

| Connector | Typical plug/housing | Wall window W×H mm | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------------|--------|
| micro-USB Type-B | ~10.6 × 8.5 shell | **13.0 × 8.5** | Overmold + insert angle |
| USB-C | ~9 × 3.2 | **12 × 7** | Cable overmold height |
| microSD path | card 15×11×1 | **16.0 × 3.5** | Slot housing |
| STEMMA QT / JST-SH | ~6–8 mm | **10 × 8** one / **22 × 9** pair | Cable bend |
| 2.1 mm barrel DC | Ø5.5 plug | **Ø8–9** | Strain relief |

State z0 from floor (board stack). Wrong **edge** is worse than slightly large windows.

Tag every opening: `from-user` | `connector-standard` | `assumed-fit-check`.
